PRIMARY SOURCE

Instagram Messaging → CRM

Connect each client's Instagram professional account. New inbound DMs can create or update the client lead automatically.

Instagram messages0Inbound message events received
Ad-attributed0Events with Meta ad referral data
Unmapped0Needs account → client mapping
Instagram webhook URLNot connected — add Supabase URL first
Important: Meta can attach referral/ad information to the message that starts the conversation. Later messages may not repeat that referral, so this CRM saves the attribution on the lead as soon as it arrives.
Instagram account mappingEach Instagram account is assigned to one client organization.
Inbound Instagram message logMessages are deduplicated by the Meta message/event ID and linked to the client lead.
